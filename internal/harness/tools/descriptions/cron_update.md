Edit an existing cron job in place: change its schedule, its command, its timeout, or its tags. Give the job ID plus only the fields you want changed; anything you omit is left as it is. Prefer this over deleting and recreating a job — recreating gives it a new ID and discards its execution history, which is the only record of whether it has been working. To pause or resume a job, use cron_pause or cron_resume instead of setting status here.
